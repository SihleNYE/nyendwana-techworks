Is a domain included?

No. Domains are registered and renewed separately. We can set one up and manage the technical side for you.

What counts as a small update?

A reasonable change to existing text, prices, an image, contact details or business hours. New pages, redesigns, features and major content are quoted first.

Can I move an existing website?

Yes. We check the site and its migration needs, then confirm suitability and any once-off migration cost before moving it.

What if a payment is overdue?

We contact you first. Continued non-payment may result in suspension until the account is up to date.
