Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Nyendwana Techworks respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, why we collect it and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you send an enquiry, you may choose to provide your name, business or organisation name, phone or WhatsApp number, the service you are interested in and a message about your needs.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, understand the work you need and communicate about a potential project. We do not sell your personal information or use it for unrelated marketing.

How enquiries are handled

Enquiry forms on this website are processed by FormSubmit, a third-party form-delivery service. Information you submit is sent to Nyendwana Techworks so that we can reply. FormSubmit may process your information according to its own privacy practices.

Sharing your information

We share information only when it is necessary to provide a service you have requested, when you ask us to do so, or when we are required to do so by law. We do not share enquiry details with third parties for their own advertising.

Keeping information safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information we receive. No online service can guarantee absolute security, so please avoid sending highly sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Your choices

You may ask us to update or delete the personal information you have sent us, or ask what information we hold about you. Contact us using the website enquiry form and include enough detail for us to identify your request.

Other websites

This website links to services such as Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and KasiBooked. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or services change. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
